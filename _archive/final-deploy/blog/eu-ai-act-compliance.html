EU AI Act Compliance: What Finance Teams Need to Know

August 2026 deadline approaching. Here's what audit trails, attestation, and documentation you need to prepare now.

The August 2026 Deadline

The EU AI Act comes into full force in August 2026. For companies using AI in any capacity that touches EU citizens, this means new requirements for documentation, audit trails, and accountability.

What Finance Teams Must Prepare

  • Audit Trails: Every AI decision must be traceable. Who authorized it? What data went in? What came out?
  • Cost Attribution: AI systems must have clear ownership and budget accountability
  • Documentation: Technical documentation of AI systems, including risk assessments
  • Human Oversight: Proof that humans are reviewing high-risk AI decisions
