Security at Finault

How we protect your data and maintain trust.

Data Processing

Client-Side Processing

Your invoice data never leaves your browser. All processing happens locally on your device. We cannot see your data even if we wanted to.

When you upload an invoice to Finault's analyzer:

  • The file is processed entirely in your browser using JavaScript
  • No invoice data is transmitted to our servers
  • The Close Pack is generated locally and downloaded directly to your device
  • We only collect anonymous usage metrics (page views, feature usage)
