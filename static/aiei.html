AI Economic Identity Envelope

An open specification for AI transaction receipts. Every AI decision gets a sealed, verifiable economic identity — who made it, what it cost, and proof it happened.

View on GitHub

The Problem

AI has no receipts.

When your company makes an AI call, there's no standard record of what happened. No receipt. No chain of custody. No way to prove what model was used, what it cost, or who authorized it. AIEI fixes that.

The AI Economic Identity Envelope is an open data format that wraps every AI transaction in a verifiable, portable receipt. Think of it as a W-2 for AI decisions — a standardized record that any system can read, verify, and audit.

The Envelope

Four sections. Complete identity.

Every AIEI envelope contains four sections that fully describe an AI transaction: who triggered it, what happened, what it cost, and cryptographic proof it occurred.

Identity — WHO

Agent ID, organization, principal (human or autonomous), environment, and session context.

Decision — WHAT

Action taken, model used, provider, protocol version, confidence score, and reasoning chain.

Economics — WORTH

Cost in USD, tokens consumed (input + output), latency, outcome data, and cost center allocation.

Integrity — PROOF

SHA-256 hashes (input, output, seal), signature, sequence number, previous hash link, and optional blockchain anchor.

Design Principles

Built for the real world.

1

Portable

JSON-based. Works with any language, any platform, any AI provider. No vendor lock-in.

2

Verifiable

SHA-256 hashes on every field. Any party can independently verify the integrity of any envelope without trusting the issuer.

3

Chain-linked

Every envelope references the hash of the previous one. After thousands of transactions, you have an unbroken chain that proves completeness.

4

Exportable, not replayable

You can always export your data. But the chain — the sequence, the hashes, the history — lives in the system that created it. You own the data. The chain is the moat.

5

MIT Licensed

Fully open. Anyone can implement AIEI in their own systems. The standard gets stronger as more companies adopt it.
