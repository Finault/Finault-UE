Preparing for FinOps for AI Certification

The FinOps Foundation's new AI certification launches March 2026. Here's what you need to know and how to prepare.

What is FinOps for AI?

The FinOps Foundation is launching a dedicated certification track for AI cost management in March 2026. This recognizes that AI workloads have unique characteristics that require specialized approaches.

The 12 Capabilities

The certification covers 12 key capabilities:

  • AI Usage Visibility
  • AI Cost Allocation
  • AI Budgeting & Forecasting
  • AI Optimization
  • AI Governance
  • AI Anomaly Detection
  • AI Chargeback/Showback
  • AI Reporting
  • AI Compliance
  • AI Benchmarking
  • AI Data Quality
  • AI Unit Economics

How Finault Maps to the Certification

Finault covers all 12 capabilities out of the box. See our FinOps certification page for the complete mapping.
