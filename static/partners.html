Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the Design Partner Program

Who can apply to the Design Partner Program? +

Any organization currently running significant AI/ML workloads or cloud infrastructure is eligible. We're looking for companies with $100K+ annual spend and a commitment to optimizing their cloud economics.

Is there a cost to join the program? +

No, the Design Partner Program is completely free. We view it as a collaboration where both parties benefit. You get early access and priority features, and we get invaluable feedback that shapes our product.

What's the time commitment? +

Minimal but meaningful. Typically 2-3 hours per week including our weekly 30-minute sync, Slack discussions, and feedback on prototypes. The time investment pays dividends in feature prioritization.

How long is the program commitment? +

We ask for a minimum 6-month commitment to ensure continuity and real impact. After that, you're welcome to continue or graduate to regular customer status.

Can I share information about being a design partner? +

Absolutely! We love when partners share their involvement. Early access and partnership are great differentiators. We'll work with you on the case study and any public materials.

What if my company's needs change? +

We understand business priorities shift. Let's talk! We're flexible and can adjust the partnership scope or transition you to a standard customer plan if needed.

How are design partners selected? +

We review applications on a rolling basis. We look for organizations with significant cloud spend, a willingness to collaborate closely, and use cases that align with our roadmap. We typically accept 3-5 new partners per quarter.
