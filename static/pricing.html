Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans anytime?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime. When upgrading, you'll be charged the prorated difference. When downgrading, the change takes effect at your next billing cycle.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) through Stripe. Enterprise customers can pay via invoice.
Is there a free trial?
Yes! Pro and Business plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.
What's included in the Gateway?
The Gateway is a real-time proxy that sits between your code and AI providers. It enables budget enforcement, automatic cost tagging, and usage tracking without code changes.
Do you offer discounts for startups?
Yes! We offer 50% off for early-stage startups (pre-Series A). Contact us at startups@finault.ai.
