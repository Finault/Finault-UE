Questions

Common questions.

Is the free audit really unlimited?
▼
Yes. Upload as many CSVs as you want, as often as you want. No account needed, no limits. The free audit runs entirely in your browser — we don't track usage because we never see your data.
What file formats do you support?
▼
Billing CSVs from AWS Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, OpenAI, Google Vertex AI, and Anthropic. We detect the format automatically — just drag and drop.
What's in a close pack?
▼
A sealed ZIP containing four artifacts: an audit PDF with full reconciliation, a GL journal entry CSV ready for your ERP, a close certificate with cryptographic signatures, and a JSON manifest with SHA-256 hashes for every file. Hand it to your CFO or auditor as-is.
Is my data secure?
▼
Your billing CSV is parsed entirely in your browser. It is never uploaded to any server. The free audit requires zero network requests — your data stays on your machine from start to finish.
What's the difference between Free Audit and Continuous Close?
▼
The Free Audit is on-demand — you upload a CSV and download a close pack whenever you want. Continuous Close automates this: we generate close packs on a monthly schedule, monitor for anomalies between closes, consolidate multiple providers, and deliver ERP-ready journal entries automatically.
How much does Continuous Close cost?
▼
Pricing depends on the volume of AI spend you manage and the number of providers. We work with each customer individually. Email bernard.cotter@finault.ai to start a conversation.
