Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? We have answers.

What file formats do you support?
▼
AWS Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, and OpenAI billing CSVs. Automatic format detection handles column order variations, different date formats, and provider-specific SKU naming conventions.
What's included in the free tier?
▼
Full audit with anomaly detection, FOCUS compliance scoring, spend-by-model breakdown, and downloadable close pack. No signup required. Just upload your CSV and download your results.
What's in a close pack?
▼
Reconciliation statement, GL journal entry, close certificate, and cryptographic manifest. All SHA-256 hashed and HMAC-signed. You get a sealed ZIP archive that's tamper-evident and independently verifiable by your auditor.
Is my data secure?
▼
Your CSVs are processed on Cloudflare Workers at the edge. No data is stored on centralized servers. Close packs are sealed in R2 with no delete operations, ensuring immutability and compliance with audit requirements.
Can I cancel anytime?
▼
Yes. No contracts, no commitments. Cancel your plan anytime from your dashboard. Your close packs remain permanently sealed and verifiable.
