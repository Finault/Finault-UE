Privacy Policy

How we handle your data with transparency and respect.

Effective Date: February 15, 2026

Introduction

Finault ("we", "us", "our") operates finault.ai. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data. We are committed to transparency and minimal data collection. We believe privacy is a fundamental right, and we've designed our platform to collect only what we absolutely need to provide our service.

Information We Collect

We collect different types of information depending on how you use Finault:

  • Billing CSV Data: When you upload your billing data to generate a close pack, we process it in-memory on Cloudflare Workers during the request lifecycle. This data is never stored after close pack generation is complete and is never shared with third parties.
  • Email Address (optional): If you provide your email for lead capture or to receive product updates, we store it in our database. You can request deletion of your email address at any time by contacting us.
  • Usage Analytics: We use privacy-respecting analytics tools that do not use cookies and do not track personally identifiable information. We collect only aggregate page view counts and feature usage metrics to understand how the platform is being used.
  • Anonymized Benchmark Data: When you run an audit, we collect anonymized, aggregated data points to power industry benchmarks and trend reports. This includes: spend size bucket (e.g., "$100K–$250K" — never the exact amount), model names and their percentage of total spend, provider distribution, FOCUS 1.3 compliance score, anomaly types detected, month-over-month change percentage, region count, and whether frontier or efficiency models are in use. We never store your company name, file name, account identifiers, or any information that could identify you or your organization. This data is used solely to produce aggregate benchmarking insights (e.g., "companies in your size bracket typically spend X% on GPT-4o"). You may opt out by contacting us at bernard.cotter@finault.ai.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for specific, limited purposes:

  • To generate close packs and audit reports from your billing data
  • To send product updates if you have opted in (you can unsubscribe from communications at any time)
  • To improve the platform based on aggregate usage patterns and user feedback
  • To produce anonymized industry benchmarks, trend reports, and pricing intelligence (e.g., "State of AI Spend" reports) based on aggregated data from all users — never from individual companies

Data Storage & Security

We take data security seriously and use multiple layers of protection:

  • Close packs are stored in Cloudflare R2 as sealed, write-once objects that cannot be modified after creation.
  • Edge computing: All processing occurs on Cloudflare Workers at the edge (300+ global locations), minimizing data transit and centralization.
  • HMAC-SHA256 signing: Every close pack is cryptographically signed to ensure tamper-evident integrity and authenticity.
  • No centralized database: Your billing CSV data is never stored in a traditional database. It exists only in memory during processing.

Third-Party Services

We use a minimal set of trusted third-party services:

  • Cloudflare: We rely on Cloudflare for infrastructure, edge computing, and R2 storage. Your data is processed and stored within Cloudflare's secure infrastructure.
  • Supabase: If you provide your email address, it is stored in a Supabase database for lead capture and product updates.
  • No advertising networks, no tracking pixels, no data brokers: We do not share your data with advertising networks, tracking services, or data brokers.

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of your email from our database at any time.
  • Right to access: Request a copy of any data we hold about you.
  • Right to opt-out: Opt out of marketing communications at any time with a single click.
  • Data requests: Contact us at privacy@finault.ai for any data-related requests, and we will respond within 30 days.

Data Retention

We maintain a simple data retention policy:

  • Billing CSVs: Are not retained after close pack generation.
  • Close packs: Are retained for the duration of your subscription, after which they can be deleted upon request.
  • Email addresses: Are retained until you request deletion or unsubscribe from communications.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or other factors. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We will notify users of material changes via email if we have your email address on file.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, how we handle your data, or if you wish to exercise any of your data rights, please contact us at privacy@finault.ai. We're here to help and will respond to all inquiries promptly.
