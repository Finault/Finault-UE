How it works

1

Route through Finault

Change your AI provider base URL to api.finault.ai. Your requests flow through our gateway — zero code changes beyond the URL.

2

Every call gets sealed

SHA-256 hashes the request and response. Chains to the previous seal. Signs with HMAC-SHA256. Stores immutably. Adds <2ms latency.

Anyone can verify

Every seal has a public receipt URL. Recompute the hash, verify the signature, check the chain. Trust math, not databases.
